## 3. Methodological Rule for Risk Calibration

1. **Qualitative vs. Quantitative Integrity:**
   * In [`verified_disease_observations.csv`](file:///d:/curuma/environmental_data/verified_disease_observations.csv), records with numerical PDI (`QUANTITATIVE_PDI`) and qualitative extension alerts (`QUALITATIVE_ADVISORY_ALERT`) are explicitly demarcated to prevent treating subjective advisory ratings as continuous mathematical numbers.
2. **Temporal Window Integration:**
   * Any calibration between these records and the ERA5-Land reanalysis dataset (`TURMERIC-ENV-TN-ERA5-2021-2023`) must operate across the **14-day antecedent exposure window ($\mathbf{W}_{\text{14d}}$)** corresponding to the verified monthly/fortnightly window, rather than an arbitrary single-hour match.
